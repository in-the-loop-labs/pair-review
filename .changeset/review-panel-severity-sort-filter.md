Sort and filter AI suggestions by severity in the Review panel. The filter menu now has a "Sort AI suggestions" choice (file order or severity, most severe first) and "Show in panel" checkboxes for Critical, Medium, Minor and no-severity suggestions. The filter only affects the panel; inline suggestions in the diff still show. The sort is remembered across reviews, the hidden severities are saved per review, and a notice at the end of the list counts the suggestions hidden from it. The menu now scrolls when it would not fit below its button.
